## Geometry, coverage, and orientation limits

**Pluto and Charon.** PDS New Horizons collection DOI [10.26007/qc8b-6p65](https://doi.org/10.26007/qc8b-6p65), monochrome LORRI/MVIC mosaics from the 2015 encounter. Native rasters are 24,888 × 12,444 and 12,693 × 6,347 at 300 m map posting. Effective observational resolution varies substantially; posting is not an assertion of uniformly measured 300 m detail. Source black southern gaps are retained and disclosed as unobserved, not dark terrain. Reference spheres are 1,188.3 km and 606 km; there is no measured relief. Each has 8,385 vertices and 16,128 triangles with normal UV seam/pole duplication. Their open index topology is a texture seam, not a terrain hole.

Projection and pixel origins are validated directly against independent pinned PDS XML: planetocentric positive-east longitude, Pluto center 180 degrees, Charon center **0 degrees**, Cartesian +X=0 longitude, +Y=90 east, +Z=north. A provisional shared-center assumption would have rotated Charon geography 180 degrees; it was corrected before qualification, and the regression reads the XML rather than repeating generator constants.

Precise map-to-epoch meridian registration remains **unqualified**. The [2021 New Horizons supporting information](https://repository.hou.usra.edu/bitstream/handle/20.500.11753/1798/2021je006964-sup-0001-supporting%20information%20si-s01.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=2), page 10, describes a 1.45-degree historical frame offset and then-uncorrected map products. The current PDS4 label describes a minimal PDS3 migration; it does not establish whether these mosaic bytes use the subsequently corrected prime-meridian realization. The map grid is qualified, but no guessed 1.45-degree correction is applied. `cartographicGridQualified=true`, `geodeticRegistrationQualified=false`, and `orientationAtEpochQualified=false` preserve this distinction. The independent IAU/PCK11 rotation evaluator does not resolve the map-edition ambiguity. NASA VTAD Pluto/Charon models were rejected for this presentation because their blended coverage and map realization were undocumented.

**Ceres.** The [NASA VTAD Ceres model](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/ceres-3d-model/) supplies the mapped oblate visualization. Original geometry, UVs, node transforms, materials and samplers are preserved byte-for-byte; only embedded imagery is downsampled. Original base color is 4096 × 2048. Source bounds are approximately 946 × 873 × 946 source units. Uniform reference scaling preserves oblateness; per-vertex sphere normalization would destroy it. Native source units, datum, per-pixel coverage, and prime-meridian registration are not published, so this is explicitly a mapped NASA visualization, **not** measured Dawn terrain or geodetically registered imagery.

**Vesta.** The [Dawn gravity-team ICQ0128 DSK](https://naif.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/naif/DAWN/kernels/dsk/dawn_vesta_grv_icq0128_v1.bds) retains 99,846 original kilometer vertices and 196,608 facets. Maximum origin-centered radius is 292.212621 km; axis extents are 575.21952 × 555.25922 × 463.28681 km. Source center is legacy SPICE 2000004, frame IAU_VESTA/10099, surface 20101; runtime identity remains the current SBDB 20000004. The DSK is the reduced ICQ128 product derived from Dawn imagery, not the full 50 m source terrain. Its comments require [dawn_vesta_grv221108_v1.tpc](https://naif.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/naif/DAWN/kernels/pck/dawn_vesta_grv221108_v1.tpc), SHA `bb17489f6d933124b502a3a9a5e3491a0d94ab34bb06a7addfa760bd8c681278`; the manifest requires the matching `dawn-vesta-grv221108-v1` model. Neutral shading avoids inventing an unregistered texture.

**Eros.** The PDS NEAR/Gaskell Q64 model retains 25,350 original listed kilometer vertices and 49,152 facets; unused source seam vertices remain preserved. Axis extents are 32.65842 × 16.88344 × 11.95893 km, maximum origin-centered radius 17.629186 km. It is never rescaled to the old mean-radius sphere. The original NASA Eros asset remains available unchanged in its existing pack. The source [eros_alex.tpc.txt](https://sbnarchive.psi.edu/pds4/non_mission/gaskell.ast-eros.shape-model/document/eros_alex.tpc.txt), SHA `0b410adcbd14bc6f678781524050b979714c7f64acd8693786f35acaa1363673`, binds the model to `PDS_GASKELL_EROS` / `pds-gaskell-eros-alex`.

Vesta/Eros source-frame coefficient evaluation was independently compared by the science reviewer with CSPICE; the asset tests bind those exact source digests to `backend/tests/fixtures/solar_destinations_frames.json`. This qualifies evaluation of the published model, not modern observational accuracy of an extrapolated prime meridian.

**67P.** Rosetta OSIRIS DLR SPG SHAP4S 100K retains all 49,993 original vertices and 99,990 facets in native kilometers and `CHEOPS_REF_FRAME_V1`. Axis extents are 5.07443 × 3.71697 × 3.04591 km. The August–September 2014 northern observations constrain the south less well. The original mesh is consistently oriented and closed with each edge in two facets; Euler characteristic is -2 (two handles), preserved rather than silently repaired. Neutral shading; no qualified epoch rotational phase or invented color map.

**Halley.** The [PDS Stooke/Abergel source label](https://sbnarchive.psi.edu/pds4/non_mission/small_bodies.stooke.shape-models/data/1682q1halley.xml) supplies a Giotto/Vega reconstruction at a 5-degree longitude/latitude radius grid. Its 2,701 tabulated samples yield 2,522 unique vertices and 5,040 facets after identifying the repeated seam/poles. Redundant boundary rounding differs by at most 0.000001 km; the longitude-zero source radius is retained, with no averaging or interpolated radii. The source expressly warns of **0.5–1 km absolute uncertainty** and possibly exaggerated depressions. Its north axis points along the long axis toward the large end, not an asserted modern IAU pole. Neutral shading and unqualified epoch orientation preserve those limitations.

Eros, Vesta and 67P conversion preserves every source vertex/facet coordinate as float32 kilometers and original facet order; only render normals are derived. The builder checks finite coordinates, ranges, non-degenerate faces, directed winding, positive volume, closed edges and topology. Halley triangulation is a declared conversion of the measured/reconstructed radius grid, not additional high-resolution observation. Eris has no surface asset in this pack; unresolved appearance must remain an explicit application fallback.
