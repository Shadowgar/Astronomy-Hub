# External Components and Data Gaps — 2026-10-10

**SUPPORT — proposal only. No provider account, credentials, spending or deployment authorized.**

God’s Eye already supplies most map orchestration and many Earth layers. The preferred outside additions supply licensed imagery, terrain, buildings, precise measurement and science data; they do not replace the Hub-owned Viewer. Read the [adoption matrix](GODS_EYE_MASTER_ADOPTION_BLUEPRINT_2026-10-10.md) first. The four feature rows classified EXTERNAL GAP are dedicated buildings, planned flight routes, precision measurement and global label/vector data. Other rows below are external **data/rights dependencies** of reusable code or corrections possible with existing native code, not additional missing upstream features.

Official sources were checked on 2026-10-10. Documentation accessibility proves a source statement, not service entitlement, tile coverage or a working Hub integration. Prices are dated USD list prices; no provider was registered or paid. Current numeric quotas are included only where the primary page supports them. Historical [provider forensics](GODS_EYE_PROVIDER_FORENSICS_2026-10-05.md) remain the detailed source map for the 73 existing provider records; deferred providers were not all exercised again.

## Dated cost and rights facts

**Cesium ion.** Community is individual evaluation/personal/noncommercial; it lists 15 GB streaming, 1,000 imagery sessions and 1,000 Google root tiles/month. Commercial lists $149/month individual or $524/team; Premium $499/$874. Commercial allocation is 150 GB, 5,000 sessions/roots; Premium 500 GB, 10,000. Public integrations outside the organization require sales/licensing clarification; these prices do not establish Astronomy Hub’s entitlement. Eligibility also depends on organization/funding/use. [Official pricing](https://cesium.com/platform/cesium-ion/pricing/).

**Google direct 3D.** Standard global pricing lists 1,000 free monthly billable units and $6 per 1,000 in the first charged band. At **10,000 root requests/month**, assuming that band, standard non-India pricing and no other APIs/tax, the SKU estimate is **$54/month**: `(10,000 − 1,000) / 1,000 × $6`. Billing must still be enabled. Root requests/session renewal, not every child tile, govern this estimate; actual sessions must be measured. Daily quota is distinct from the monthly free allocation. [Pricing](https://developers.google.com/maps/billing-and-pricing/pricing), [billing units/quotas](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/usage-and-billing).

Google logos and per-tile content attribution must remain visible. Unauthorized caching, bulk extraction, offline reuse and analysis are restricted; normal HTTP caching is subject to policy. Public privacy/terms and restricted credentials are required. Screenshot/export and mesh-derived analysis need explicit terms review, not an assumption from successful rendering. [Map Tiles policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/policies).

**Bing is excluded from the recommended aircraft/map set.** Cesium’s content guide disallows asset tracking/fleet/routing use and prohibited map combinations; it warned of retirement availability only through September 2026. That date has passed. Current service availability and replacement rights were not tested, and Azure imagery is marked preview in ion pricing. Do not infer production permission from the pinned Bing catalog entries. [Content guide](https://cesium.com/learn/ion/content-usage-and-attribution-guide/).

**MapTiler.** The current list offers a noncommercial Free proof-of-concept plan and Flex from $30/month plus extra usage. In third-party libraries such as Cesium, usage is requests rather than MapTiler SDK sessions; a 512-pixel rendered tile can count as four requests. Included quotas/overage amounts for the selected account/product were not conclusively captured, so no monthly volume estimate is supplied. Validate them before recommending a budget. [Pricing](https://www.maptiler.com/cloud/pricing/), [cloud terms](https://www.maptiler.com/terms/cloud/).

**Esri.** Official raster basemap APIs require the appropriate account/access mechanism. A callable public World_Imagery endpoint does not prove rights to an unauthenticated public Cesium deployment. Current selected-product price and legacy endpoint permission remain unresolved; obtain the exact product terms and attribution metadata before approval. [Raster API](https://developers.arcgis.com/rest/static-basemap-tiles/), [pricing](https://developers.arcgis.com/pricing/), [service terms](https://www.esri.com/en-us/legal/terms/web-site-service), [attribution](https://developers.arcgis.com/documentation/glossary/data-attribution/).

## Ranking and choices

1. **Existing Cesium + public God’s Eye factories:** highest integration fit and least new code; no substitute for data/rights. Restore stars and tune existing appearance before optional effects.
2. **Keyless baseline:** current NASA/USGS/local fallback + controlled OSM + qualified Re:Earth improves terrain and roads economically, but cannot promise worldwide city aerial/photoreal quality. Public-service capacity is a product constraint.
3. **Qualified account raster + World Terrain:** MapTiler/Esri/ion are candidates for global imagery and labels, with clear selected-product rights, operating budget and regional quality checks required. No unsupported quality winner is declared from marketing.
4. **Google photorealistic 3D through direct or ion route:** strongest documented match to the aspirational supported-city exploration target; limited coverage, binding content rules, geocoder compatibility and GPU/usage costs prevent a universal promise.
5. **Native OSM buildings:** practical structural alternative where data exist; visual fidelity below photographic meshes, height/coverage gaps explicit.
6. **Protomaps/OpenFreeMap/vector rendering:** open data/control advantages; lower immediate integration fit for Cesium labels. Prefer established raster options before commissioning rendering work.

Owner must choose a tier, public organizational/commercial scope, fallback set, maximum usage/spend, acceptable coverage and geographic quality floor. The [visual decision packet](EARTH_VISUAL_FOUNDATION_DECISION_PACKET_2026-10-10.md) compares all three tiers without treating a free allocation as public entitlement. Unresolved rights, AeroAPI pricing, Mapillary terms, exact Esri/MapTiler selected-account quotas and Gaia official retrieval remain explicit research gates. No missing value is invented.
