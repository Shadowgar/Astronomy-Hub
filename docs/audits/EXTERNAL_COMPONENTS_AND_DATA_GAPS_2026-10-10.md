# External Components and Data Gaps — 2026-10-10

**SUPPORT — proposal only. No provider account, credentials, spending or deployment authorized.**

God’s Eye already supplies most map orchestration and many Earth layers. The preferred outside additions supply licensed imagery, terrain, buildings, precise measurement and science data; they do not replace the Hub-owned Viewer. Read the [adoption matrix](GODS_EYE_MASTER_ADOPTION_BLUEPRINT_2026-10-10.md) first. Other rows below are external **data/rights dependencies** of reusable code or corrections possible with existing native code, not additional missing upstream features.

Official sources were checked on 2026-10-10. Documentation accessibility proves a source statement, not service entitlement, tile coverage or a working Hub integration. Prices are dated USD list prices; no provider was registered or paid. Current numeric quotas are included only where the primary page supports them. ## Ranking and choices

1. **Existing Cesium + public God’s Eye factories:** highest integration fit and least new code; no substitute for data/rights. Restore stars and tune existing appearance before optional effects.
2. **Keyless baseline:** current NASA/USGS/local fallback + controlled OSM + qualified Re:Earth improves terrain and roads economically, but cannot promise worldwide city aerial/photoreal quality. Public-service capacity is a product constraint.
3. **Eligible zero-cost account raster + terrain:** only permitted free products are candidates; ion Community remains conditional. Written club/public rights, no charges, quotas and regional quality checks are required. No unsupported quality winner is declared from marketing.
4. **Photorealistic 3D research:** preserve existing Google direct/ion integration findings. Direct billing and paid ion are inadmissible; Community 3D remains conditional on written club/public rights and $0 protection. Coverage, content/geocoder restrictions and physical budgets still apply.
5. **Native OSM buildings:** practical structural alternative where data exist; visual fidelity below photographic meshes, height/coverage gaps explicit.
6. **Protomaps/OpenFreeMap/vector rendering:** open data/control advantages; lower immediate integration fit for Cesium labels. Prefer established raster options before commissioning rendering work.

Owner spending is fixed at $0.
