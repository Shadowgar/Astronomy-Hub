Block unclear cable/model/launch/street-level rights and latest-only token paths. Nothing rejected or deferred was removed in this audit.

Measurement needs a precise refinement: the historical record correctly reports no standalone measurement UI. Pinned `src/annotations/drawMode.js` already has `pathLengthM` (spherical distance), `ringAreaM2` (local planar approximation), antimeridian handling and display formatting. Global ellipsoidal accuracy or terrain-following distance requires the external/native option and an explicit error model. This is an expanded source finding, not a silent rewrite of historical evidence.
